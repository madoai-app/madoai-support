title: MadoAI Privacy Policy
---


Last updated: 13 October 2026

## Summary

**MadoAI collects nothing about you.**

Your companion's conversation, voice and motion are made on your smartphone or PC.
What you type, the companion's replies and your settings are not sent to anyone,
including the developer (except when you choose to send a report; see "Reporting a reply" below). There is no account to create.

**The exceptions are Google Play and Steam, which deliver the app.** On a smartphone,
the app receives its AI model files from Google Play. At that point Google's mechanism
sends device information and the app version to Google (see "Smartphone" below).
**The developer never receives any of it.**

## What is not collected

None of the following is collected, received, or stored:

- Name, email address, phone number, or any contact details
- Location
- Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
- Usage analytics or crash reports
- What you type, or the companion's replies
- Your voice (the app does not use the microphone)

## What stays on your device

The app works with the following, and **none of it leaves your device** (except when you choose to send a report; see "Reporting a reply" below).

| What | Where | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What you type and the companion's replies | Inside the app | To make a reply and show it on screen |
| Settings | Smartphone: app storage / PC: the storage folder (see below) | To determine behaviour |
| AI model files (language, voice, motion) | Smartphone: app storage / PC: the data folder placed with the app | To make replies, voice and motion on the device |

## Smartphone (Android)

The app **does not have the permission to connect to the internet (INTERNET).**
Conversation, voice and motion are all made on the device.

**The AI model files are received from Google Play after installation.**
Google Play performs the download. The app asks for it, and receives its progress,
through Google's Play Asset Delivery mechanism. According to Google, this mechanism
sends **device information and the app version** to Google in order to deliver the
right files. Google states that this data is encrypted, is not transferred to third
parties, and is deleted after a fixed retention period
([Google's description](https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/asset-delivery)).
For how Google handles data, see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## PC (Windows)

The PC version is distributed through Steam. Steam handles the download and updates,
and that handling is governed by
[Valve's privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).

- The AI that writes the replies runs as a separate program on the same PC. The app
  connects to it **only within the PC (127.0.0.1)**, and it cannot be reached from outside.
- The app blocks outgoing connections (http, https, ws, wss) from its screen.
- Settings and other data are stored by default in a folder under `%APPDATA%`. You can
  change this in the "保存先" (storage location) section of the settings screen. A small file that
  remembers the storage location is also kept under `%APPDATA%`.

## The AI voice

The companion's voice is synthetic speech generated by AI. The app's settings screen
says so as well.

## Reporting a reply

You can flag an inappropriate reply with "報告" (Report) next to it. The reply is marked and recorded on your device.
Pressing "開発者へ送る" (Send to developer) opens a GitHub Issues page pre-filled with the report
(app version, time, what you said just before, and the reply).
**Whether to send it is your choice.** The app never sends it by itself. You can review and edit the text in your browser before posting.
Posting to GitHub Issues requires a GitHub account, and **what you post is public.**

## Sharing with third parties

None. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or SDKs, with the one
exception of Google's mechanism for receiving the model files from Google Play,
described above.

## Deleting your data

- **Smartphone**: Deleting the app removes the settings and model files in app storage
  from the device.
- **PC**: The storage folder is outside the app's folder. To remove your data, delete that
  folder. The current storage location is shown in the "保存先" (storage location) section of the settings
  screen.

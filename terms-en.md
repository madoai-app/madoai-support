## Article 4. Voice replacement (importing a reference recording)

1. The app can import an audio file of a voice and make the companion's voice sound closer to it
   ("voice replacement"). Only one of the following may be imported:
   - **your own voice**
   - **a voice whose owner has given you permission that states the use, period and scope** (a "permitted voice")
2. At the start and at the end of an import, the app asks you to confirm the following. The import does not proceed until you confirm all of them.
   - Whether the voice you import is your own voice or a permitted voice (you choose one)
   - For a permitted voice, that the owner of the voice has given you permission, stating the use, period and scope, to use it as the companion's voice in this app
   - That you are 18 or older
   - That the recording is a file recorded by the owner of the voice (or with the owner's agreement), and is not cut from a broadcast, video, stream, game or the like
   - That you will follow the prohibited uses in Article 5
3. Together with the imported recording, the app stores a **consent record** on your device. The consent record holds
   the date and time, the version of these terms, the statements you confirmed, **whether the voice is your own or a permitted voice**,
   for a permitted voice **that you confirmed you have the permission**, a value calculated from the imported recording (a hash),
   and a random ID that the app creates the first time. No device identifier is used. The permission document itself is not put into the app; keep it yourself.
4. The imported recording and the consent record are **stored only on your device and are not sent off it.**
   The developer does not receive them. The app does not use a voice whose consent record is missing or does not match the recording.
5. You can export and delete an imported voice from the app's settings. Deleting it also deletes its consent record.
6. Use a permitted voice only within the use, period and scope of the permission. When the period ends,
   or when the owner of the voice withdraws the permission, delete that voice from the app.
7. The developer cannot see or hear the recordings you import.
   You are responsible for the recording being your own voice or a permitted voice, and for how you use it.

When you use the app, you must not:

1. **Import the voice of anyone other than yourself without the permission of the owner of the voice.** This applies to anyone — family, friends, celebrities, voice actors, streamers, people who have died — if you do not have the owner's permission stating the use, period and scope, you must not import it. This includes using a voice beyond the scope of the permission.
2. **Impersonate another person.** This includes using the app's voice or replies to make others believe that you or the companion is a specific real person. This applies to a permitted voice too.
3. **Use the app for fraud or any other crime, or to help one.**
4. **Use the app for sexual purposes.** This includes using a voice made with the app in sexual audio content.
5. **Use the app for political purposes.** This includes using a voice made with the app in election campaigning or in promoting political messages.
6. **Treat a voice that comes with the app (a preset voice) as your own voice, or as the voice of a specific real person.**
7. Publish or distribute audio made with the app while falsely presenting it as not made by AI.
8. Use the app to harass or bully anyone.
9. Break the law, or infringe the rights of others (including rights in their voice or name, privacy, and copyright).
10. **Modify or reverse-engineer the app to remove its safety mechanisms, such as the confirmations and the consent record, or distribute an app with those mechanisms removed.**

An AI companion app that lives on your smartphone or PC.

## Contact

- GitHub Issues: https://github.com/madoai-app/madoai-support/issues

Please send bugs, requests and questions there. Japanese or English is fine.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need an account?

**No.** There is no account to create to use the app.
The Google Play or Steam account you use to get the app belongs to that store.

### I cannot talk right after installing (smartphone)

After installation, the app receives from Google Play the AI model files that make the
companion's words, voice and motion. Until that finishes, a waiting screen shows the
progress.

If the download stops with a failure, tap **"もう一度"** (retry) on the waiting screen.

### Does it work without an internet connection?

The smartphone app does not have the permission to connect to the internet.
Conversation, voice and motion are made on the device. Only receiving the model files
uses Google Play's connection.

On a PC too, conversation, voice and motion are made on the PC.

### Whose voice is the companion's?

It is synthetic speech generated by AI. The app's settings screen says so as well.

### Where does my conversation go?

**Nowhere.** What you type and the companion's replies are handled only on your device.
The exception is when you choose to send a report about an inappropriate reply: that report (the reply and what you said just before) is posted publicly to GitHub Issues.
See the [privacy policy](privacy-en.html) for details.

### I want to change where settings are stored (PC)

Use the **"保存先"** (storage location) section of the settings screen. The new location is
used from the next time you start the app.

### What happens if I delete the app?

- Smartphone: the settings and model files in app storage are removed from the device.
- PC: the storage folder is outside the app's folder, so it remains. To remove it, check
  the location in the "保存先" (storage location) section of the settings screen and delete
  that folder.
